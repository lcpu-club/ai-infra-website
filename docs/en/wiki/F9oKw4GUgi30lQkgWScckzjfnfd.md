Why are supercomputing and AI infrastructure so closely related? Why have
advances in compute enabled increasingly capable AI systems?

Large models run and train effectively on GPUs because, like scientific
computing and climate simulation, their workloads can be highly parallel.

- Why can these workloads run in parallel?
- Where does parallelism appear?
- How do we express and execute parallel computation?
- Why does AI infrastructure require custom kernels, while ordinary CPU
  programming is rarely described in the same way?

This session develops the intuition needed to answer those questions.
